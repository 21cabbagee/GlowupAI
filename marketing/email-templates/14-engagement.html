Tips for Specific Skin Concerns

Tracking Acne:

  • Focus on the "Breakouts" metric - track the count over time
  • Note what you ate or stress levels in the notes field
  • Compare photos weekly to see healing progress

Tracking Redness/Rosacea:

  • The "Redness" metric is your key indicator
  • Take morning photos (redness often worse in morning)
  • Test products one at a time (2 week experiments minimum)

Tracking Aging/Texture:

  • Focus on "Texture" and "Tone Evenness" metrics
  • Progress is slower - compare monthly, not weekly
  • Retinoids take 8-12 weeks to show results

Tracking Hydration:

  • Track "Hydration" metric after applying products
  • Compare AM vs PM hydration levels
  • See which moisturizers actually work
