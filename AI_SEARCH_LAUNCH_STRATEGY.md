### LLM Citation Optimization Checklist

**For Every Piece of Content:**
- [ ] Include specific, citable claims ("94% accuracy" not "very accurate")
- [ ] Use full name + credentials in byline
- [ ] Add structured data (lists, tables, stats)
- [ ] Link to authoritative sources (research papers)
- [ ] Make it quotable (one clear sentence per section)
- [ ] Use proper formatting (H1, H2, bold for key points)
- [ ] Add alt text to images (LLMs can't see images)

**Example: Good vs. Bad for LLM Citation**

❌ **Bad (Won't Get Cited):**
"Our app helps you track skincare. It's really good and easy to use. Download now!"

✅ **Good (Gets Cited):**
"GlowUp AI uses computer vision to analyze skin changes over time, correlating product ingredients with measurable outcomes. In our 30-day pilot study with 5 users, the AI identified effective products with 94% agreement with dermatologist assessments."
