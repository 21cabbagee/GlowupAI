Take Your First Photo

💡 Tips for Your First Photo

  • Lighting: Natural light from a window works best (avoid direct sunlight)
  • Timing: Morning is ideal (consistent time = better comparisons)
  • Clean face: No makeup for your baseline (you can track makeup separately later)
  • Position: Face the camera directly - our guide will help you align

What happens next?

Take photos every 2-3 days for the first month. Why? Because skincare changes happen gradually - you won't see overnight results, but you WILL see trends over weeks.

After 2-3 weeks, you'll have enough data to:

  • Compare your progress
  • See which metrics are improving
  • Start running experiments on products

Remember: This is cosmetic tracking, not medical diagnosis. For any medical skin concerns, please consult a dermatologist.
