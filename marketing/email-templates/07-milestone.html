What's next?

📊 Try Comparison Mode

You now have a week of photos! Go to the Insights tab and tap "Compare" to see your Day 1 vs Day 7 side-by-side.

Don't expect huge changes yet - skin improvements take 4-6 weeks. But you might notice subtle shifts in redness, texture, or breakouts.

🧪 Start Your First Experiment

Have a product you want to test? Set up your first experiment:

  1. Go to Routine → Experiments
  2. Choose a product to test
  3. Set a hypothesis (e.g., "Vitamin C will reduce redness")
  4. Set duration (2-4 weeks recommended)
  5. Track the results!

This is how you'll discover what truly works for you.
