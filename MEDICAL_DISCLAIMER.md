## 4. Appearance Metrics Limitations

### 4.1 Factors Affecting Accuracy

The appearance metrics generated by GlowUp AI (e.g., redness score, blemish count, texture score) are influenced by many factors, including:

- **Lighting conditions:** Different lighting can drastically alter the appearance of your skin in photos
- **Camera quality:** Phone camera resolution, lens quality, and sensor capabilities vary
- **Photo angle and distance:** Inconsistent positioning affects metric comparability
- **Environmental factors:** Makeup, skincare products, sweat, dirt, or image compression
- **Algorithmic limitations:** Computer vision models have inherent accuracy limitations and may misinterpret visual data
- **Model version changes:** Updates to our algorithms may cause metrics to change over time

### 4.2 Not Diagnostic Tools

**Appearance metrics are NOT diagnostic tools.** They cannot:
- Detect skin cancer, melanoma, or other serious medical conditions
- Diagnose dermatological diseases (e.g., rosacea, eczema, psoriasis, acne vulgaris)
- Replace clinical examination by a dermatologist
- Measure medically relevant parameters (e.g., transepidermal water loss, sebum production, pH levels)

### 4.3 Confidence Scores

Metrics include a "confidence" score that reflects the algorithmic certainty of the measurement. **Low confidence does not mean medical concern; it means the photo quality or conditions made analysis difficult.** Similarly, high confidence does not guarantee medical accuracy.

## 9. Specific Disclaimers

### 9.1 Not for Diagnosis or Screening

**GlowUp AI is NOT a tool for diagnosing or screening for:**
- Skin cancer (melanoma, basal cell carcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma)
- Precancerous lesions (actinic keratosis)
- Dermatological diseases (rosacea, eczema, psoriasis, dermatitis, acne, fungal infections)
- Allergic reactions or contact dermatitis
- Autoimmune conditions affecting the skin (e.g., lupus)
- Infectious diseases (bacterial, viral, or fungal infections)
- Any other medical condition

**If you notice any concerning changes in your skin, see a dermatologist immediately. Do not rely on GlowUp AI to detect or rule out medical conditions.**

### 9.2 Skin Cancer Awareness

**GlowUp AI cannot detect skin cancer.** If you notice any of the following, consult a dermatologist immediately:
- **A**symmetry: One half of a mole doesn't match the other
- **B**order: Irregular, blurred, or jagged edges
- **C**olor: Multiple colors or uneven color distribution
- **D**iameter: Larger than 6mm (size of a pencil eraser)
- **E**volving: Changes in size, shape, color, or elevation over time

**Early detection of skin cancer saves lives. Regular dermatological skin exams are essential, especially if you have risk factors (family history, sun exposure, fair skin, many moles).**

### 9.3 Acne and Medical Conditions

If you have persistent or severe acne, cystic acne, or acne that does not respond to over-the-counter treatments, **consult a dermatologist.** Severe acne may require prescription medications (e.g., isotretinoin, antibiotics, hormonal treatments) that only a licensed healthcare provider can prescribe.

GlowUp AI's blemish count metric is a cosmetic observation, not a clinical acne severity assessment (e.g., not comparable to the Investigator's Global Assessment scale used in clinical trials).

### 9.4 Sensitive or Compromised Skin

If you have sensitive skin, a compromised skin barrier, or existing dermatological conditions (e.g., eczema, rosacea, perioral dermatitis), **consult a dermatologist before making significant routine changes.** What works for others may not work for you and could worsen your condition.
