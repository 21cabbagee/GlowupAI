## Messaging Framework

### Core Value Proposition
"Track what actually works for YOUR skin with scientific rigor and complete privacy."

### Key Messages
1. **Scientific:** A/B testing, metrics, evidence-based
2. **Privacy-first:** Local storage, user control, no sharing
3. **Effective:** See real changes over weeks/months
4. **Habit-building:** Streak system, consistency

### Differentiation
- **vs Curology:** No prescription, test any products
- **vs SkinVision:** Cosmetic tracking, not medical
- **vs generic photo apps:** Automatic metrics, experiments
- **vs instant AI apps:** Long-term tracking, real progress

---

## Brand Voice

**Be:**
- Encouraging and supportive
- Scientific but accessible
- Honest about limitations
- Privacy-focused
- Celebrating small wins

**Don't be:**
- Pushy or salesy
- Making medical claims
- Promising overnight results
- Spammy or desperate
- Corporate/sterile

**Example good tone:**
"You've been tracking for a week—that's huge! Real skincare results take 4-6 weeks. Keep going!"

**Example bad tone:**
"Amazing transformation in 3 days! Upgrade now to unlock!"
