3 Tips to Get Accurate Results 💡

Hi there!

You've been using GlowUp AI for a few days now - great start! Here are 3 expert tips to make sure your tracking data is as accurate as possible:

1 Same Time of Day

Morning light is best. Take your photos within the same 1-2 hour window each time. Why? Lighting changes throughout the day, and your skin looks different at different times.

Pro tip: Set a daily reminder for your ideal capture time (we recommend right after your morning routine).

2 Same Position Every Time

Use the face overlay guide. Our app shows you exactly where to position your face. Match your previous position as closely as possible.

Pro tip: Stand in the same spot each time (same distance from window, same background). Muscle memory helps!

3 No Makeup for Baseline

Clean, bare skin shows real changes. While you can track makeup looks separately, your core tracking photos should be makeup-free to see true skin improvements.

Pro tip: Take your photo right after cleansing, before applying any products (except if you're testing a specific product's immediate effect).

Remember: Consistency is KEY.

The more consistent your photos are, the more accurate your metrics will be. Small changes in lighting or position can affect the readings, but our face detection helps minimize that.
